Core capabilities

Build only where AI earns a place in the process.

The solution shape depends on the documents, decisions, systems, and controls involved.

DI

Document intelligence

Classify mixed documents, extract structured fields and tables, validate against rules, and route uncertainty for review.

  • PDFs, scans, forms, invoices, statements
  • Structured JSON, Excel, database, or API output
WA

AI Workflow Automation

Connect intake, AI processing, deterministic rules, approvals, and downstream actions into a traceable sequence.

  • Routing, drafting, validation, and hand-offs
  • Human approval at consequential steps
KA

Knowledge assistants

Help teams find and use approved internal information with source-aware answers and access controls.

  • Policies, procedures, product knowledge, and SOPs
  • Retrieval, citations, permissions, and feedback
BA

Bounded AI agents

Design agents for clearly defined tasks, tools, stopping conditions, escalation paths, and evaluation.

  • Specific goals and permitted actions
  • Logs, limits, human review, and fallbacks
