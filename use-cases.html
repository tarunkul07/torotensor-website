Business-function use cases

Start with the work—not the AI buzzword.

The same technology creates different value, risk, and learning needs across departments. These examples connect AI capability building with carefully selected workflow automation opportunities.

Discuss Your Team’s Priorities

These are opportunity patterns, not promises of automatic outcomes. Each requires discovery, approved data handling, realistic evaluation, and clear human responsibility.

Department opportunity map

Enable people. Improve workflows. Keep judgment where it belongs.

Finance & compliance

Review-ready financial work

Teams can learn
Analysis, reconciliation support, reporting, scenario exploration, and responsible use with sensitive information.
Workflows to explore
Invoices, receipts, statements, expense packs, field extraction, validation, and structured exports.
Human control
Tax treatment, unusual transactions, low-confidence fields, policy exceptions, and final approvals.
Measures
Review time, extraction quality, exception rate, rework, and adoption.
HR & people operations

AI with confidentiality and judgment

Teams can learn
Policy drafting, employee communication, qualitative analysis, learning content, and administrative support.
Workflows to explore
Candidate packs, onboarding forms, policy retrieval, document completeness, and employee-query routing.
Human control
Consent, sensitive personal data, fairness, employee relations, hiring decisions, and final communication.
Measures
Response consistency, administrative effort, completeness, escalation quality, and user trust.
Sales & customer operations

Consistent preparation and follow-through

Teams can learn
Account research, meeting preparation, proposal drafting, follow-ups, and customer-conversation analysis.
Workflows to explore
Enquiry classification, call summaries, CRM field suggestions, response drafts, and next-action routing.
Human control
Pricing, commitments, sensitive cases, relationship judgment, and any final external message.
Measures
Response time, CRM completeness, preparation time, follow-up consistency, and conversion-quality signals.
Procurement & supply chain

Documents connected to decisions

Teams can learn
Supplier research, comparison, risk summaries, reporting, and structured communication.
Workflows to explore
Purchase orders, invoices, delivery notes, specification comparison, three-way matching, and exception routing.
Human control
Supplier selection, commercial terms, quantity and price variances, policy exceptions, and approvals.
Measures
Match quality, exception cycle time, document handling effort, late action, and rework.
General operations

Knowledge turned into repeatable action

Teams can learn
Research, planning, SOP drafting, meeting-to-action workflows, analysis, and reporting.
Workflows to explore
Request intake, document classification, policy lookup, status reporting, and cross-team hand-offs.
Human control
Priority conflicts, unusual requests, policy interpretation, resource decisions, and final ownership.
Measures
Cycle time, hand-off quality, unresolved requests, consistency, and adoption across teams.
Technical, data & product

Reliable AI application engineering

Teams can learn
APIs, structured outputs, retrieval, agents, evaluation, observability, security, and deployment patterns.
Workflows to explore
Internal assistants, document pipelines, developer support, product intelligence, and bounded tool-using agents.
Human control
Architecture, access, release approval, failure handling, security decisions, and production changes.
Measures
Task quality, error modes, latency, cost, escalation, traceability, and maintained performance.

Choose the right starting point

Not every use case needs software.

Some problems are solved by better team practice. Others benefit from a small controlled pilot. The first decision is what kind of intervention the work actually needs.

AI Capability Building

When the main gap is knowledge, confidence, quality, or responsible use.

Workflow redesign

When the process itself needs clearer steps, ownership, and review.

Workflow automation pilot

When repeatable inputs, rules, value, and human controls can be defined.

Advisory

When leadership needs help prioritizing, governing, or sequencing investments.
