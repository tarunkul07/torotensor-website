Privacy

How website enquiries are handled.

This notice explains the limited information involved when you contact ToroTensor through this website.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Information you choose to provide

When you contact ToroTensor, you may provide your name, work email, company, role or department, approximate team size, area of interest, and a description of your business challenge.

How the enquiry form works

The form on this website prepares an email in your device’s email application. It does not display a false confirmation or claim that a separate website database has received your message. The enquiry is sent only when you review the prepared email and press Send.

How we use enquiry information

ToroTensor Technologies Pvt Ltd uses the information to understand and respond to your enquiry, arrange relevant discussions, prepare proposals you request, and maintain appropriate business correspondence.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell enquiry information. Information may be handled by service providers that support ordinary business email and communication, subject to their applicable terms. Correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, business records, legal obligations, and dispute prevention.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete personal information contained in an enquiry, subject to applicable legal or record-keeping requirements. You can also ask us not to contact you further.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@torotensor.com or write to ToroTensor Technologies Pvt Ltd.
