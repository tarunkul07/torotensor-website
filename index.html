FAQ

Common questions before a guided demo.

We scope each engagement around the workflow, document types, output requirements, review needs, integrations, and deployment choice.

Which teams are the best fit?

Document-heavy and workflow-driven teams handling recurring PDFs, spreadsheets, emails, records, approvals, or manual follow-ups.

Will it replace staff?

No. The product is designed to support staff with capture, drafting, reminders, tracking, and reporting while keeping humans in control.

How is this different from general AI agents?

The starting point is a defined business input and a verifiable output, supported by validation rules, review checkpoints, and controlled downstream actions.

Can we start without full integrations?

Yes. A pilot can start with sample data, forms, spreadsheets, email exports, and documents. Deeper integrations can be added later.
